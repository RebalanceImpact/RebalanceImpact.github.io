About Rebalance Impact

Our Experience

Our team of Chartered Accountants and Environmental Scientists blends climate research, financial expertise and environmental data to deliver practical sustainable solutions that connect technical insights with strategic business goals across industries.

Industry Experience

We have worked with industry leads across a variety of sectors, including mining, energy, finance, and property. Our scope extends beyond JSE listed companies and beyond South African focused reporting. Exposure to SOX controls in an ESG environment, and working directly with UN and the Dutch National Government for data analytics and visualization.

Environmental Data & Analytics

Demonstrated expertise in tracking, processing, and analyzing climate commitments from over 10,000 companies worldwide, providing valuable insight into diverse climate strategies and pathways. Expertise in developing, managing, and visualizing large-scale datasets, covering areas such as energy use, biodiversity, water withdrawals, and social development indicators. Responsible for data management and visualizations in two upcoming chapters of the UN Global Environmental Outlook report.

ESG Assurance & Advisory

Extensive experience in ESG assurance for industry leaders, advisory work for first-time CSRD reporters, and assurance readiness engagements, across regulatory frameworks like GRI, Responsible Sourcing, and CSRD. With water management experts, we also offer full, independent water balance assessments.

Why Rebalance Impact Was Founded

The ESG space is rapidly evolving, especially in the Global North, with new regulations and standards emerging at an unprecedented pace. South African companies, particularly those in exporting heavy industries, will need to adapt to these changes to stay competitive in international markets.

Small and medium enterprises (SMEs) often face tougher challenges in terms of affordability and complexity when trying to keep up with these regulations and maintain competitiveness through voluntary reporting. The barrier to entry can be overwhelming, leaving many businesses struggling to navigate the complex landscape of sustainable reporting.

At Rebalance Impact, we recognized this gap and were driven by our passion for sustainability while understanding the critical importance of business growth and competitiveness. We believe that sustainable business practices and financial success are not mutually exclusive – they can and should work hand in hand.

Our mission is to make ESG reporting accessible, affordable, and strategic for businesses of all sizes, ensuring that South African companies can thrive in an increasingly sustainability-focused global marketplace while contributing meaningfully to environmental and social progress.

Why Us?

We are an intimate, dedicated firm committed to understanding the unique life of your business. This deep understanding allows us to offer personalized, unparalleled flexibility, crafting tailor-made solutions and fostering truly collaborative relationships.

Our distinctive expertise blends both accounting rigor and environmental sciences. With years of experience in financial and non-financial accounting and environmental policy development, we are perfectly positioned to navigate and lead the evolution of non-financial (ESG) reporting, ensuring your business is not just compliant, but thriving sustainably to optimize an ever-evolving future.
