Key Frameworks

Reporting Standards We Work With

We help organizations navigate the complex landscape of sustainability reporting frameworks.

GRI

Global Reporting Initiative

The most widely used sustainability reporting standards globally.

ISSB

International Sustainability Standards Board

IFRS S1 and S2 - New global baseline for sustainability disclosures.

SASB

Sustainability Accounting Standards Board

Industry-specific standards focused on financial materiality.

TNFD

Task Force on Nature-related Financial Disclosures

Framework for reporting on nature-related dependencies, impacts, risks and opportunities.

CSRD

Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive

EU directive requiring comprehensive sustainability reporting from companies.

CSDDD

Corporate Sustainability Due Diligence Directive

EU directive on environmental and human rights due diligence.

OECD

OECD Guidelines for Multinational Enterprises

International standards for responsible business conduct.

CDP

Carbon Disclosure Project

Environmental disclosure system for companies and cities.

UN SDGs

UN Sustainable Development Goals

Framework for aligning business impact with global goals.
