Your Guided Path to ESG Reporting

Your ESG Journey begins with a comprehensive Gap Assessment. We will work with you to understand what standards are applicable to you - from GRI and TCFD to local regulations - identify your reporting boundaries - establishing which legal entities, business units, and activities are in scope - and conclude on the objectives of your reporting needs - compliance readiness, reporting alignment, or performance improvement.

We conduct a Double Materiality Assessment to identify key impacts and opportunities, establishing which ESG topics are most significant for your business and stakeholders. Our approach ensures that you collect data specific to your company’s risks, opportunities, and industry standards, aligned to the KPIs identified in earlier assessments.

We then guide you in choosing and capturing the right ESG Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)—from carbon emissions and resource use to governance and social metrics. We create detailed criteria supported by Sustainability Data Guidance Reports that instil a robust reporting and data capture process for your company.

With the metrics defined, we support you by centralizing your ESG data, implementing controls for accuracy, and ensuring all information flows smoothly across teams. Our systems and quality checks make data collection reliable and audit-ready. Utilizing robust analytics tools, we can transform your data into actionable insights and visuals.

We help you prepare disclosures that meet both mandatory and voluntary requirements. This ensures you’re aligned with industry standards and regulatory expectations, ready for informed report creation. Your ESG story becomes clear and compelling, supporting internal awareness and external trust with boards, investors, and regulators.

Finally, once you are successfully reporting and want to enhance the reliability of your reporting through obtaining assurance, your data and controls will already be ready for a successful audit. We will ensure that the burden of the audit does not impact the day to day operations of your company by engaging directly with your auditors to lead you to a clean audit opinion.
