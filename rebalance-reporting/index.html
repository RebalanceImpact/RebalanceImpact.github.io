How we draft your report

  1. 01

    Learns your voice

    Upload past reports, your website, company profile, brand guidelines and data. No impact report yet? Start with what you have already published.

  2. 02

    Answers become the draft

    Quick, guided questions about your initiatives go to the right people. Their answers and stories flow into your first draft.

  3. 03

    Grounded in your data

    Every figure is linked to its source, so your story holds up when people ask. Anything missing is requested from your team, never estimated.

How your team works

  1. 04

    One portal. Your whole team.

    Invite colleagues and give each the right access. Everyone gets their chapters and due dates.

  2. 05

    Edit your report

    Edit text, figures and images in place. Change a figure once and it updates everywhere it appears.

  3. 06

    Review and sign off

    A clear status for every page, from draft to final, through to sign-off. Our reporting experts review every report with you.
