Our Methodology

How We Handle Your Data

A structured approach to data management and analysis that ensures accuracy, transparency, and actionable outcomes from our insights.

Data Collection

At Rebalance Impact, we begin by centralizing and collecting non-financial and financial data into a unified platform. This consolidation eliminates silos and ensures data integrity to support accurate tracking and informed, integrated decision-making.

Target Setting

We help you establish transparent, measurable target setting processes that build stakeholder trust and enable clear tracking of your financial and non-financial performance progress.

Forecasting

Model the impact of any operational changes or desired targets to analyze potential impacts on financial and non-financial KPIs. Our scenario analysis provides insights into cost-effectiveness and helps align sustainability goals for long-term business performance.

Shared Accountability

We promote collaborative data governance by aligning internal teams and suppliers around shared accountability for data quality, driving continuous improvements and strengthening financial and sustainable performance across your entire value chain.

Automation

Less Time on Routine Work

Data capture, reconciliations and reporting can take days every month. We automate these repetitive tasks so your team can focus on what matters.

Utility & Fuel Data Capture

Electricity, water and fuel figures are captured from bills and fuel cards each month and converted to emissions, ready for reporting.

Supplier Data Requests

Supplier questionnaires are issued, followed up and collected on a schedule, with responses checked as they arrive.

Reconciliations

Bank and ledger lines are matched automatically, leaving your team to review only the exceptions.

Invoice & Document Capture

Invoices and supporting documents are read, validated and coded automatically, with duplicates flagged before they post.

Expense Claims & Approvals

Receipts are captured from email or a phone photo, checked against policy and routed for approval before they post to the ledger.

Inventory & Reordering

Stock on hand is matched against upcoming jobs and orders, and reorders are raised in time, so you never over-order or run short.

Data Checks & Alerts

Missing, late or unusual figures are flagged as they arrive, so issues are resolved before they reach a report.
